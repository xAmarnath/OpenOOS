OpenOOS / iQOO 13 I2401 - experimental Android 17 userspace guest
Source snapshot: 6 November 2026.

See README.md for the current feature matrix and docs/DEVELOPMENT.md for limitations.
Earlier sessions reached Home, Settings and About, displayed without blinking,
connected Wi-Fi, produced camera previews and sensor events, and changed brightness.
These earlier observations do not establish stability of the latest revision.

Latest guest unlock-key encoding fix: implemented; end-to-end testing pending.
Screen lock, storage/download saving, hardware attestation and Google login:
end-to-end validation remains open. Google sign-in has been reported failing.
No-SIM modem tests do not prove calls, SMS or mobile data work. Audio untested.

Guest images, binaries, APKs, account data and prepared data seed are not in Git.
Reboot clears temporary root/runtime mounts but retains guest data files.
Sessions have no fixed time limit; manual stop and failure recovery remain.
The guest shares the stock kernel/vendor stack and is not a VM security boundary.
Private bundle export does not sanitize personal data and must not be published.
