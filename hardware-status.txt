OpenOOS / iQOO 13 I2401 - experimental Android 17 userspace guest
Source snapshot: 6 November 2026.

See README.md for the current hardware matrix.
Display, touch/gestures, Wi-Fi, camera preview, sensors and brightness work.
Shared storage, downloads, hardware-backed app attestation and Google login work.
Bluetooth discovery works; pairing and transfer remain untested.
NFC enables/disables; no tag available for a transaction test.
GNSS reports satellites; no location fix obtained during the test.
Audio uses a dummy backend without a physical speaker route.
Screen-lock setup remains unresolved. No-SIM tests do not verify calls or data.

The switcher app displays the current OS and one switch button.
Guest images, binaries, APKs, account data and prepared data seed are not in Git.
Reboot clears temporary root/runtime mounts but retains guest data files.
Sessions have no fixed time limit; manual stop and failure recovery remain.
The guest shares the stock kernel/vendor stack and is not a VM security boundary.
Private bundle export does not sanitize personal data and must not be published.
